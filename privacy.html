Privacy Policy for PitchMe

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Welcome to PitchMe ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use the PitchMe mobile and web application.

1. Information We Access

PitchMe provides AI-powered voice interview coaching and communication feedback. To deliver these services, the app requests the following access:

  • Microphone / Audio Recording: We access your device microphone exclusively when you tap "Record" during practice sessions. This audio is processed locally or securely evaluated to analyze communication metrics such as clarity, pacing, filler words, and relevance.
  • Practice Data: Your practice history, attempt scores, streak counts, and selected categories are stored locally on your device to track your personal improvement.

2. How We Use Your Audio

Audio recorded in PitchMe is used solely to generate feedback on your interview delivery and communication structure. We do not sell, rent, or trade your voice recordings or personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your practice attempt scores and user preferences are retained locally on your device using encrypted storage mechanisms. You may reset or clear your practice history at any time by clearing the application's local storage or app data.

4. Children's Privacy

PitchMe does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
